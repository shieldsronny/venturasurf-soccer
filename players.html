The Ventura Surf Way — Our Four Pillars

Everything at Ventura Surf is built on the same four pillars — every team, every age, every week. It is the curriculum our coaches coach from and the standard our players are measured against, and it is unique to our club.

Technical

What a player can do with the ball — ball control, passing and first touch, shooting, heading, and 1v1 skills.

Tactical

How a player reads and plays the game — positional play, decision-making, attacking and defensive movement, and set pieces.

Physical

The engine behind the game — endurance, speed, strength, agility, and flexibility, built for the demands of the match.

Mental

The mindset — focus, composure, leadership, adaptability, and competitive spirit, on and off the field.
