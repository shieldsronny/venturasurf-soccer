Session Ideas by Age Group

One club, one weekly focus — but it should look different at every age. Here's how to run this week's theme (check the Coaching Focus above) with your group, U5 through U19.

U5–U7 — FUNdamentals

The theme at this age: "Running with the ball" is just dribbling with big touches into open space. No patterns yet — every player with a ball, moving the whole time.

Try: Red Light / Green Light with a ball each · Sharks & Minnows across the channel · Follow-the-Leader dribble tag.

Coaching point: Praise touches and bravery, not goals. No lines, no elimination games.

U8–U10

The theme at this age: Dribble at speed with the head up, plus the first simple combination — pass it and follow your pass.

Try: Gate Race (dribble through as many gates as you can in 30 seconds) · Numbers Game 1v1 / 2v1 to end lines · Pass-and-Follow squares in groups of 4.

Coaching point: Big touch into space, eyes up between touches.

U11–U13

The theme at this age: The full weekly focus — run with the ball to break lines, and pattern play (Y pattern, third man) as in the session plans above.

Try: 4v2 / 5v2 rondos with a dribble-out condition · Pattern-to-finish waves · 3v1 keep-away progressing to 4v2.

Coaching point: Scan before the ball arrives; run to commit a defender, then release it.

U14–U16

The theme at this age: Carrying the ball becomes a line-breaking tool, and patterns become build-out and third-man combinations under real pressure.

Try: 6v4 build-out against a mid-block with dribble-or-pass decision gates · Third-man finishing circuit · Transition waves, 3v2 into 4v3.

Coaching point: Timing — carry it until the defender steps to you, then play the free player.

U17–U19

The theme at this age: Game-model work — breaking lines inside the team's shape, rehearsed build-out patterns, and pressing traps.

Try: Phase-of-play build-out vs a press (10v8 or 11v11) · Counter-press reaction game after a dribble turnover · Set-piece and second-ball patterns.

Coaching point: Decisions at game speed — speed of play and constant communication.
